The year is 1071. Sultan Alparslan's raiders ride ahead of the army toward the plain of Manzikert (Malazgirt). Set out with a handful of riders, grow your army along the road and scatter every army that stands in your way. Then ride on for nine centuries: Nicaea, Myriokephalon, Kosovo, Varna, Constantinople, Mohács, Szigetvár, Crete, Prut, Belgrade, Acre, Plevna. And at level 1453, reach the end of the Road to Conquest.

Swipe left and right and pick the right side at every gate: plus and times grow your army, minus and divide melt it. As the army grows, the road widens from two lanes to four and your raiders speed up. Earn 16 bows, from the Plain Bow to the World Bow, and put Golden Alps at the head of your army.
